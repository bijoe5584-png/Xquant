QUANT GALAXY v2 — Interactive Financial Engineering Architect Mastery App

Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
No installation or internet connection required. Progress saves to browser localStorage.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- Curriculum: 13 modules x 4 lessons = 52 deep lessons (theory, key ideas,
  formula, worked numeric example, pitfalls, build task, interview proof),
  aligned to the WQU MScFE path plus quant platform architecture.
- Formula Deck: 36 formulas grouped by category, each with meaning + interview angle.
- Flashcards: flip-and-grade drill mode; unknown cards resurface first.
- Mastery Test: 48-question bank with per-answer explanations; random 12-question
  draws, filterable by module; best score tracked.
- AI Quant Crew: 6 deterministic offline coaching agents sharing a 34-topic
  knowledge base (keyword-matched) plus rotating drills. No API keys, works offline.
- Interview Lab: 4 rounds x 8 questions (32 total) with rubric scoring and a
  model-answer outline revealed after each attempt.
- Job Simulator: 10 trading-floor incident missions with debriefs.
- Visual Atlas: 5 full-size mastery posters.
- Readiness Signal: weighted progress (lessons 40%, assessment 25%,
  interviews 20%, simulator 15%) with per-domain constellation tracking
  and a reset button.

Project-evidence sections reference: Quant Alpha Foundry, the regime-aware
XLE/XOP pairs-trading capstone, the LSTM forecast study, live trading
infrastructure (OANDA / Interactive Brokers), and the 10-asset optimizer.
